Summary card — “What you’ve told PC Chat™”

The card is drawn at a fixed 359 × 350. Everything inside it except the summary prose is fixed too, so the prose has a hard height budget — and therefore a hard character budget, which the generator that writes this text has to respect.

The 46 chars/line figure is derived from the drawn copy itself: paragraph one is 280 characters across exactly 6 lines, paragraph two 163 across 4. That is 443 characters in 10 lines — 96% of the ceiling, so the design as drawn has almost no headroom. Character width varies with content (numbers and capitals are wider), so generate to about 430 and truncate rather than let the card grow.

The summary and the memory list are two separate representations

highcontent-model

'What you've told PC Chat™' is a written paragraph set covering pregnancy, blood pressure, lisinopril, a peanut allergy, a son's cold, province, family physician, a COVID-19 vaccine and an MRI. 'Top of mind' below it lists the same facts as discrete items. Nothing on the frame says whether the paragraph is generated from the list, authored separately, or refreshed on a different cadence — and 'Last updated today' is attached only to the summary.

Ask Is the summary derived from the memory list, and does editing or deleting a memory regenerate it?
