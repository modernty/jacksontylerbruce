Surfaces

6

In the side panel

A shortlist under Recents, reachable from anywhere, for picking a recent conversation back up. Three content states: empty, the everyday four, and the shortlist at capacity.

The Chat history page

The full list: counted, searchable, each row carrying its age and an overflow control. Five content states: four chats, twelve, twelve scrolled, fifty behind a pager, and none at all.

Renaming a chat

The row menu's first option. An alert over the list with the keyboard up, prefilled with the existing title. Three states: opened, cleared, and the renamed row.

Deleting one chat

The row menu's second option, gated behind an alert that is explicit about memories outliving the conversation. Three states: the menu, the gate, and the toast.

Deleting several

Select turns every row into a checkbox and the nav into Select all and Cancel. Eight states walk from nothing picked through a counted delete, an all-chats delete, and the empty page that follows.

Search

Search replaces the title bar rather than sitting under it. Six frames walk the character bands: inert under the threshold, a spinner while the query runs, matches with the matched run bolded, and a silent spell correction past seven characters.
