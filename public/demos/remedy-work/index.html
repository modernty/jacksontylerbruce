Future state explorations

First hello test

Open

Onboarding polish was cut from launch and the character explorations never got a verdict, so the first minute runs on a default line. The agent opens with a register chosen on purpose, warmth arriving in the same breath as competence, and a first question that proves it listened.

Open→

Loyalty scan test

Prototype ready

Ninety percent of Canadians live within ten kilometres of a store, and acquisition is still imagined as campaigns. The Optimum scan becomes the first conversation, and one tap opens an agent that already knows the points balance and the pharmacy.

Open→
Open→

Connection test

Prototype ready

The connection is one tap and almost nobody takes it, because the ask arrives before any proof. Value gets shown before permission is asked, then paid off immediately by an answer that knows the dosage and the timing.

Open→
Open→

Handoff test

In flight

The stated fear about one agent is a health question answered with a product and read as an upsell. The health answer completes first, the product arrives as the means to the plan, and the privacy wall is visible at the moment it matters.

Open→
Open→

Mode control test

In flight

One agent with a health side and a shopping side needs the difference to be legible without anyone reading a manual. The shift announces itself the first time it happens, and leaving always takes one visible tap.

Open→
Open→

Clinical signal test

Prototype ready

A retailer giving health advice is presumed to be selling until proven otherwise. One calm signal carries both promises, real medicine and protected data, in plain words rather than stacked badges and lock icons.

Open→
Open→

Prescription doorway test

Open

A new prescription is the highest intent moment in the business and today it ends with a leaflet. The pickup becomes the first step of a care plan, what changes, what to watch for, when to come back.

Open→
Open→

Family profile test

In flight

Dependents are the highest value context in the product and the hardest thing to ask for directly. A kid mentioned in passing becomes a confirmable card in the moment, so capture feels like being heard rather than being filed.

Open→
Open→

Proof test

In flight

People test the agent for memory on purpose and watch for a thumb on the scale. Personalization shows its work, recall arrives inside the task it helps with and recommendations carry their reasoning.

Open→
Open→

Canadian trust test

Open

The real competitor is the generic assistant already on the phone. Provincial coverage, Canadian guidelines and data that stays in the country get named right in the answer, where a global assistant has nothing to say back.

Open→
Open→

Lifestyle surface test

In flight

Nothing today gives a healthy person a reason to open the product, so retention is being asked of a surface built for sick days. A surface that reflects the person back, plans mid flight, the family, one thing worth doing today.

Open→
Open→

Programs test

Open

People treat the agent as a starting point they leave to finish elsewhere. The answer becomes a program, the next seven days proposed unasked, one step per day with a visible finish line.

Open→
Open→

Head start test

Open

The switching cost is not features, it is history, and every month it goes untested the incumbent's head start compounds. Connection and profile give the agent real context before the first question is finished.
