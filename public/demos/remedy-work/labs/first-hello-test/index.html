Question 01 · First hello test · Earning first use

How does the agent introduce itself so the first chat starts a relationship?

OpenRegister test · New to Remedy Canadians, AI wary quota seated

The hypothesis

People decide in the first exchange whether this is a someone or a something, and they tell a someone more. In health, warmth has to arrive in the same breath as competence. Charm alone reads as a toy. Pure clinical reads as a form with better manners.

What we’re testing

The register of the first minute, warm, clinical, or brisk, measured by what people volunteer in turn two rather than what they say they liked.

Why we’re asking

Onboarding polish was cut from launch and the character explorations never got a verdict, so the first minute is currently running on default. The first hello also sets the ceiling on disclosure for everything after it, which makes it an adoption question wearing a brand costume.

How we’d run it

Three hellos with identical capability. The professional, credentialed and calm. The companion, warm and on a first name basis. The concierge, brisk and capable. Everyone meets all three in random order.

A disclosure read after each hello, measured by what people volunteer in their next turn rather than by what they say they liked. Volunteering is the honest signal.

A comeback read a day later. Which hello they remember, and whether they describe it as the app or as a someone.

What this settles, stated plainly. The register, not the copy. The final script is a writing pass that comes after the register is chosen.
