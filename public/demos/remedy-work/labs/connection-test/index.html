Question 05 · Connection test · Personalization and memory

What value convinces someone to connect their health data, and what do they get back?

Prototype readyAsk frame test · Wearable and Apple Health owners who shop SDM

The hypothesis

The connect ask fails because it arrives before any proof. Show one visibly better answer built on connected data, a preview, a sample, a named wall the agent cannot get past without it, and the tap follows. Value has to be visible before the permission is requested.

“Not just Remedy knowing their medications, but really going so far as knowing and incorporating things like their dosage, the timing of their medications, knowing ahead of time if anything with our meds have changed.”

Research finding

What we’re testing

The value shown before the ask, and the visibly better answers after it. Includes whether people even know which records they have and what connecting does.

Why we’re asking

The connection is one tap and almost nobody takes it. Connected labs and vitals change answer quality in a step, not a slope, which makes this single permission moment the growth lever for the entire personalization stage. Nobody has tested what the ask should look like.

The prototype

  1. 1Mid conversation, the agent names what it cannot see.
  2. 2A preview shows the shape of a connected answer.
  3. 3The consent sheet says in plain words what is shared.
  4. 4The first connected answer proves the trade was worth it.

How we’d run it

Three asks, traded off by every person. The cold ask as it exists today. The preview ask, a sample of what connected answers look like. The earned ask, three turns in, where the agent names the wall. With your labs I could answer this properly.

Measure the tap and the comprehension together, because a connection someone does not understand is a revocation waiting to happen. People explain back what is shared and with whom.

A revoke read. Whether knowing exactly how to disconnect raises willingness to connect, which is what autonomy research predicts.

What this settles, stated plainly. The design of the ask. It does not settle whether people keep the connection over months, which is a live pilot question.

On the card

The connection is one tap and almost nobody takes it, because the ask arrives before any proof. Value gets shown before permission is asked, then paid off immediately by an answer that knows the dosage and the timing.
