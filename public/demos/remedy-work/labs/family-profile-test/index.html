Question 04 · Family profile test · Personalization and memory

Can the agent build a family profile without ever showing a form?

In flightConcept test · Parents and caregivers managing two or more people

The hypothesis

People will not fill out family forms, but they mention family constantly in passing. If a mention becomes a confirmable card in the moment, my son Adam has diabetes becomes a profile with one tap, then capture feels like being heard instead of being filed.

“They also wanted a medication manager, a lab results explainer, proactive condition tracking, and profiles for dependent family care.”

Research finding

What we’re testing

A kid mentioned in passing becomes a confirmable profile the agent can use. Capture through conversation, with the consent line found rather than guessed.

Why we’re asking

Dependents are the highest value context in the product and the hardest thing to ask for directly. Booking for family is one of the top jobs. Forms are historically where family features go to die, and the consent question, who confirms what about whom, has no pattern anywhere to copy.

The prototype

  1. 1A parent mentions their son in passing.
  2. 2An inline card offers to remember him, one tap.
  3. 3A later booking asks, is this for Adam.
  4. 4The family view in the menu shows what the agent holds.

How we’d run it

The conversational capture prototype in front of real caregivers. Mentions become inline confirmation cards. The first measure is comprehension. Do people know a profile now exists, without being told to check.

The correction moment, staged on purpose. The agent gets one detail wrong. We watch whether fixing it builds trust, which ownership research predicts, or breaks it.

The guardian question. Testing who can see this framing at the moment of creation against the moment of first use, to find where the consent line has to sit.

What this settles, stated plainly. The capture and confirm pattern. It does not settle the full household permission model, which is deep enough to be its own study.
