Question 11 · Lifestyle surface test · Earning loyalty

What makes a health surface worth opening on a day you feel fine?

In flightWeek long field test · PC Health users, mixed engagement

The hypothesis

Symptom less opens come from a surface that reflects the person back. Their plans mid flight, their family, one thing worth doing today. Recognition is the pull, not information. Content feeds are where health apps go to be ignored.

“For most people it didn't feel like it had these really compelling differentiators to draw loyalty yet.”

Research finding

What we’re testing

The surface that reflects the person back. Plans mid flight, family, one next step. The reason to open it on the first visit and still on the fifteenth.

Why we’re asking

The retention story depends on healthy day opens, and nothing in the product today gives a healthy person a reason to look. The smart surface work already exists to port, and the feed alternative has failed publicly enough times that testing before building is the whole point.

The prototype

  1. 1The morning surface reflects the household back.
  2. 2A plan card shows the week mid flight.
  3. 3A family card holds the next thing for Adam.
  4. 4One next step, and nothing else asking for attention.

How we’d run it

Two surfaces, one week each. The mirror. Your plans, your people, one next step. The feed. Tips, articles, seasonal content. Same participants, both weeks, order randomized.

Count the symptom less opens and what followed. An open that leads to a completed step is worth more than three that lead nowhere.

An emptiness test. What the mirror shows a brand new user before there is anything to reflect, because the cold start is where mirror surfaces usually die.

What this settles, stated plainly. The surface model. It does not settle the card taxonomy, which follows once the model wins.

On the card

Nothing today gives a healthy person a reason to open the product, so retention is being asked of a surface built for sick days. A surface that reflects the person back, plans mid flight, the family, one thing worth doing today.
